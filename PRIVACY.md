# Privacy Policy

Audio Ninja, for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Effective 24 September 2026

## The short version

Audio Ninja does not collect, store or share any information about you. It has no accounts,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

## Your audio files

The files you open are read and edited on your device. Saving writes them back to where they
came from; Export As writes a copy to the place you choose. Audio Ninja does not upload them,
copy them anywhere else, or keep any record of which files you opened.

If you open a file from iCloud Drive or another cloud provider, that provider syncs it the same
way it syncs any other file. That happens under the provider's own privacy policy, not through
Audio Ninja.

## Settings

Audio Ninja remembers one display preference, whether Show Separate Channels is on. It is
stored on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

## Permissions

Audio Ninja does not ask for access to your microphone, photos, contacts, location or any other
personal data. On the Mac it runs in Apple's App Sandbox and can open only the files you choose.

## Third-party code

The MP3 encoder, LAME, is built into the app and runs entirely on your device. It sends nothing
anywhere. No other third-party code is included.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may
send the developer anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects these under its
own privacy policy, and you can turn this off at any time: on iPhone and iPad in Settings ▸
Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements, and on the Mac in System Settings ▸ Privacy &
Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements.

## Children

Audio Ninja collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective
date. Every past version is kept in this repository's history.
